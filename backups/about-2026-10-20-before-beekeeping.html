Our story

A family tradition,
carried forward.

From North Carolina to Liberal, Kansas — more than thirteen years with the bees.

Hi, I’m Kyle.

I’m the beekeeper behind Snowman’s Apiary. I’ve been keeping bees for more than thirteen years, first in North Carolina and now here in Liberal, Kansas.

My dad and I started keeping bees together in North Carolina. The time we spent with our bees became an important part of our story — something we learned, worked at, and shared.

Since my dad passed away, I’ve continued that work through Snowman’s Apiary. It’s my way of carrying on what we started together and keeping his memory part of the work I do today.

Today, Snowman’s Apiary brings that family tradition to my community in Liberal. Thank you for supporting my bees, a small local business, and the work my father and I began together.
