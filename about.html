Our story

A family tradition,
carried forward.

More than thirteen years keeping bees, from North Carolina to Liberal, Kansas.

Hi, I’m Kyle.

I’m the beekeeper behind Snowman’s Apiary. I’ve been keeping bees for more than thirteen years, first in North Carolina and now here in Liberal, Kansas.

My dad and I started keeping bees together in North Carolina. We learned together and spent a lot of time working with our hives.

Since my dad passed away, I’ve kept going through Snowman’s Apiary. Taking care of these bees is my way of carrying on what we started together and remembering him.

I now keep bees here in Liberal, Kansas. Thank you for supporting my small business and the work my dad and I started together.
