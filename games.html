Queen of the Meadow

Choose the queen or snowman and survive thirty waves at Meadow’s Edge, then thirty-five at Thistle Dusk. Beat both to unlock Crownfall Arena, where overlapping bosses drop chests that combine your fusions into super weapons. Continue into Endless for bigger, faster-arriving crowds with slowly rising health and damage. Play with mouse or keyboard, or twin sticks on your phone.

How to play

Tap or click the snow to shovel. With a keyboard, focus the shovel button and use Space or Enter. Your best score is saved in this browser when storage is available.

You can pause the game or reduce snow animation and screen shake. Your browser’s reduced-motion preference is respected, too.
