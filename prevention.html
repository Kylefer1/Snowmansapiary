Prevention & thoughtful care.

Good decisions begin with observations, a record and a reason to act.

A repeatable inspection record

For each hive, note the date, weather, queen or egg evidence, brood appearance, food, space, pest results and any action. Set the next check according to what you found. Comparing a colony with its own earlier records is more useful than relying on memory.

Integrated pest management

IPM combines monitoring with cultural, mechanical and treatment tools. Examples include appropriate stock, planned brood interruption and selected registered products. Each has limits. Measure the result after an intervention and adjust the plan when necessary; “treated” and “controlled” are not the same observation.

Reduce avoidable stress

Maintain access to forage, food and water. Match space to colony strength, inspect with a purpose, and investigate weak colonies before combining them or moving frames. Keep a plan for summer food shortages as well as winter stores.

Keep problems from travelling

Avoid sharing suspect comb or feeding honey of uncertain disease status. Pest control in stored equipment does not automatically disinfect it. If a disease is suspected, get a diagnosis and a cleaning or disposal plan suitable for that organism.

Think beyond the hive entrance

Pesticide exposure can occur while bees forage. Communicate with nearby land managers, follow pollinator-protection directions on any pesticide used, and consider drift, flowering plants and bee activity. Nighttime application alone does not guarantee that a product is safe for pollinators.

Treatment choices depend on context

Temperature, brood, honey intended for harvest and the product’s use directions all matter. Follow the label on the actual registered product. Avoid copying a dose, recipe or schedule from an unrelated product or old online post.

Educational guide · Updated October 20, 2026.

Observe your own colony and local conditions. For diagnosis or treatment decisions, use qualified local advice and the current product label.
